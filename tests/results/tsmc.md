# Regression Case — 台积电

- Archetype: semiconductor manufacturing
- Raw Material (approx): 34
- Independent clusters (approx): 33
- Key new-model signal: **Wright's Law / Experience Curve — strong**
- Key new-model signal: **Capital Cycle — strong**

## Why
Scale economy explains fixed-cost dilution; Wright's Law explains cumulative manufacturing learning, yield and unit-cost improvement. Capital Cycle explains the later supply/ROIC feedback after industry-wide investment, which is distinct from strategic arms-race behavior.
